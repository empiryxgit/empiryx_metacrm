# RUTA Lead Management System — Individual User Guide

Welcome! This guide walks you through setting up and using your RUTA Lead Management System as an individual user (a single business owner or independent professional, not an agency managing multiple clients). It only covers features that actually exist in the product today — if something here doesn't match what you see on screen, please let your admin know.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating your account

1. Go to the Register page and choose **Individual / Business**.
2. Fill in your business name, your name, email, mobile number, and a password (at least 10 characters).
3. Click **Create Account**.

That's it — there's no separate step to pick an "industry" or plan. Your account is created immediately with a working lead management system behind it.

`[Screenshot: Registration form]`

### Logging in

Go to the Login page, enter your email and password, and click **Sign In**. If you check "Remember me," you'll stay signed in for longer on that device.

If you ever forget your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the login page, or ask your admin to reset it for you.

---

## 2. First-Time Setup

The very first time you log in, RUTA asks you for just one thing before taking you to your Dashboard: your **business name** and, if it's changed, your **mobile number**. That's it — one screen, well under a minute.

`[Screenshot: First-time setup screen]`

Everything else — your sales pipeline, what you call the people you track, and connecting Facebook/Instagram — is already set up with sensible defaults, or waiting for you on the Dashboard as an optional next step. Nothing about it blocks you from getting to your leads right away.

**If you close your browser before finishing:** no problem. The next time you log in, RUTA picks up exactly where you left off.

**Once you finish setup**, you won't see this screen again. Logging in from then on takes you straight to your Dashboard.

---

## 3. Dashboard

Your Dashboard is your home base — a real-time snapshot of your customers and sales, filtered by whichever date range you choose (Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, or a custom range).

`[Screenshot: Dashboard overview]`

What you'll find here:

- **Key numbers** at the top — total leads, new leads, qualified leads, and however many reached your "won" stage in the selected period.
- **A trend chart** showing new, qualified, and won leads over time.
- **Lead List** — your most recent leads in the selected period, with a link to see everything in Pipeline.
- **Funnel** — how many leads made it through each stage of your pipeline.
- **Lead Sources** — which channels (Meta, Website, Referral, and so on) your leads are coming from.
- **Recent Activity** and **Upcoming Follow-ups** — so you never lose track of who needs a call.

If your account is brand new, you'll see a friendly empty state instead of blank tables, with a link to add your first customer.

You'll also see a small **"Finish setting up your account"** checklist for a little while, if there's anything left to do — connecting Facebook/Instagram, adding your first lead, or inviting a teammate. Each item links straight to where you complete it. You can dismiss the checklist any time — it's just a nudge, not something you're required to complete.

## 4. Leads

Every person who reaches out to your business — whether from a Facebook ad, your website, or a phone call you logged yourself — becomes a **lead** in RUTA.

Leads carry: name, contact details, source, which pipeline stage they're in, which campaign (if any) brought them in, who owns them, and any follow-up date you've set.

You can view, search, and manage all of your leads from the **Pipeline** page (see below).

---

## 5. Pipeline

The Pipeline page is a visual board of your sales process — each column is a stage (like New, Contacted, Qualified, Site Visit, Won, Lost), and each card is a lead.

`[Screenshot: Pipeline board]`

- **Drag a card** to a different column to move that lead to a new stage.
- Click **+ Add Customer** to manually add someone you spoke with off-platform (a walk-in, a referral, a phone call).
- Use the search box or the table view if you prefer a list instead of a board.
- Set a follow-up date and notes on any lead so it's easy to remember when to reach back out.

If you haven't added anything yet, the pipeline shows a friendly "Your pipeline is ready" message with a button to add your first customer.

---

## 6. Campaigns

Campaigns let you organize where your leads are coming from, especially useful if you're running more than one Facebook or Instagram ad at a time.

`[Screenshot: Campaigns page]`

- Create a campaign with a name and platform (Facebook, Instagram, or both).
- If you've connected Meta (see below), your real ad campaigns can be synced in and mapped to a campaign here, so leads from that ad automatically land in the right place.
- Each campaign shows how many leads it's brought in, how many were qualified, and how many were won.

## 7. Facebook & Instagram Leads (Meta Integration)

If you run Lead Ads on Facebook or Instagram, RUTA can pull those leads in automatically — no manual downloading or copy-pasting.

`[Screenshot: Meta connection status]`

To connect:

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Meta** (or use the "Facebook / Instagram connected" item on your Dashboard's setup checklist, if it's still showing).
2. Sign in with Facebook and grant the requested permissions.
3. Choose which Page(s) and ad account you want RUTA to watch.

Once connected, new leads from your Lead Ads forms appear in your Pipeline automatically, usually within moments of someone submitting the form. You can check your connection status any time from the Integrations page, and reconnect if it ever needs attention.

Connecting Meta is entirely optional — everything else in RUTA works whether or not you ever connect it, and you can connect or disconnect at any time.

### WhatsApp leads (same connection, no extra setup)

If any of your ad campaigns are set up to send people to WhatsApp instead of a lead form ("Click to WhatsApp" ads), RUTA picks those up automatically too — through the exact same Facebook/Instagram connection above. There is nothing separate to connect, no phone number to type in, and no extra permission screen to click through: once Meta is connected, RUTA quietly checks whether you have a WhatsApp Business number set up under the same Meta account, and if it finds exactly one, it starts using it right away.

When someone messages your WhatsApp number after clicking one of those ads, a new lead appears in your Pipeline exactly like a Facebook form lead does — same table, same list, no separate "WhatsApp Leads" page to check. Open the lead and you'll see **Lead Approach: WhatsApp**, along with which ad and campaign it came from (when Meta tells us) and their first message. If the message didn't come from a trackable ad — someone messaged your number directly — the lead is still captured, just labeled **Unknown / Organic WhatsApp** instead of being left out.

If your Meta account has more than one WhatsApp number connected, RUTA will ask you to pick one from the Meta Integration status screen; this only happens when a choice is genuinely needed.

## 8. Settings

From the Settings menu you can manage:

- **Business Configuration** — your industry template (which controls your pipeline stages and lead fields) and, if you like, a fully custom template of your own stages and fields.
- **Integrations** — connect or manage Facebook/Instagram.
- **Users** *(if you invite others to help you)* — add teammates, assign roles, and control what each person can see and do.
- **Your profile and password.**

Anything set generically during setup — your business details and pipeline — can always be revisited and changed here.

---

## 9. Troubleshooting

**I don't see any leads from Facebook/Instagram.**
Check Settings → Integrations → Meta for your connection status. If it shows "Needs attention," reconnect using the same button. Make sure the correct Page and ad account are selected.

**I accidentally closed the setup screen before finishing.**
Just log back in — RUTA remembers exactly where you left off and picks up from there.

**I want to change what I call my leads (Lead/Prospect/Customer/etc.).**
This isn't configurable yet — every account currently uses "Lead." It's on our roadmap.

**I forgot my password.**
Use "Forgot password" on the Login page, or ask your admin (if you've invited teammates) to reset it for you.

## 10. FAQ

**Do I need to pick an industry when I sign up?**
No. Every account starts on RUTA's general-purpose template. You can pick a specific industry template (like Real Estate or Solar) any time from Settings → Business Configuration, or build your own custom one.

**Is connecting Facebook/Instagram required?**
No — it's entirely optional, at signup and forever after. You can add leads manually or from other sources without ever connecting Meta.

**Do I need to set up WhatsApp separately from Facebook/Instagram?**
No. WhatsApp leads use the exact same Meta connection — there's no separate WhatsApp setup step, phone number entry, or extra login. If you have a WhatsApp Business number linked to your Meta account, RUTA finds and uses it automatically once Meta is connected.

**How long does first-time setup take?**
Under a minute — it's just your business name and, if it's changed, your mobile number. Everything else (pipeline, Facebook/Instagram) is either set up for you automatically or waiting as an optional item on your Dashboard checklist.

**Will I see the setup screen again after I finish it?**
No. Once you finish setup, logging in takes you straight to your Dashboard.

**Can I invite other people to help me run RUTA?**
Yes, from Settings → Users, if you'd like a small team working alongside you.

**Is my data separated from other RUTA customers?**
Yes. Every account's data is fully isolated — no other company can ever see your leads, campaigns, or settings.
