Frequently asked questions

What is RUTA?

RUTA is a lead management system built specifically for businesses running Facebook & Instagram Lead Ads. It connects directly to your Meta ad account and pulls every lead into one shared pipeline the moment someone submits — no manual exporting from Ads Manager, no spreadsheets, no leads sitting unseen.

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. Sign in with Facebook from Settings → Integrations, pick your Page, Instagram account, and ad account — RUTA syncs your campaigns, forms, and leads automatically, and subscribes itself to receive lead events for you. There are no Page IDs, access tokens, or webhook URLs to enter.

Does RUTA support leads from WhatsApp ads?

Yes. When one of your ads is set to send clicks to WhatsApp, RUTA detects that from the ad's own configuration and captures the first message as a lead. This is ad-destination detection, not a WhatsApp inbox — RUTA doesn't replace WhatsApp Business or manage your ongoing conversations, it captures the lead the moment it arrives.

What if RUTA can't tell where an ad's leads come from?

RUTA never guesses. If an ad has no linked Instant Form and its destination isn't WhatsApp, it's flagged as "Unknown" on your Meta Integration status page rather than being assigned a lead source it can't verify.

Can my whole team use one RUTA account?

Yes. Invite your team and assign role-based permissions, and split leads by branch or location if you operate in more than one place.

What's the difference between the Individual and Agency plans?

Individual is for running your own campaigns and pipeline. Agency includes everything in Individual, plus the tools for managing multiple client accounts from one login: client-wise campaign reporting, aggregate lead reporting across clients, one-click switching into any client's own workspace, and audit-logged access.

What's included in the 15-day free trial?

Full RUTA functionality with 1 connected campaign and every lead it generates — free for 15 days. There's no feature gating on the trial; it's the same product, just capped to 1 campaign.

What's the difference between the billing cycles?

Nothing about the functionality or campaign limit changes — only how often you're billed. Committing to a longer cycle lowers your effective monthly price: 10% off on Quarterly, 20% off on Half-Yearly, and 25% off on Yearly, compared to paying monthly.

How many campaigns or clients can I connect?

Individual plans include 5 campaigns. Agency Basic includes 5 client accounts and 10 campaigns total, split across them however you choose.

Is client data kept separate on the Agency plan?

Yes. Every client's data lives in its own isolated account — agency users only ever see clients they've been explicitly assigned to, and every time an agency user accesses a client's data, it's automatically logged.
