Why teams switch to RUTA

Leads move fast. Most CRMs can't keep up.

Without RUTA

  • Someone manually checks Meta Ads Manager and copies leads into a spreadsheet — hours after they came in.
  • Duplicate entries, missing phone numbers, and no record of which ad or form actually generated the lead.
  • No one knows who's supposed to follow up, so hot leads go cold by the time someone calls.
  • Setting up a webhook meant hand-entering Page IDs, access tokens, and verify tokens correctly, every time.

With RUTA

  • Leads land in RUTA the instant they're submitted — no one has to go looking for them.
  • Every lead is automatically de-duplicated and tagged with its exact Page, ad, campaign, and form.
  • Leads route straight into one shared pipeline with clear ownership and follow-up reminders.
  • Connect once with your Meta login — no Page IDs, tokens, or webhook URLs to enter, ever.
